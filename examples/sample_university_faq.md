## Admissions

### How do I apply for an undergraduate programme?
Applications are submitted online through the Admissions Portal. Create an account,
fill in the application form, upload your documents (Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets,
a photo ID, a passport-size photograph) and pay the application fee of INR 1,500.
You will receive a confirmation e-mail with your application number within 24 hours.

### What are the application deadlines?
For the autumn (July) intake, the main application window is 1 January – 31 May.
Late applications are accepted until 30 June subject to seat availability and a late
fee of INR 500. The spring (January) intake, available for selected postgraduate
programmes, accepts applications from 1 September to 30 November.

### What are the eligibility criteria for B.Tech?
Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and
scored at least 60% aggregate in PCM. Admission is based on the university entrance
test score (70%) and a personal interview (30%). JEE Main scores are also accepted.

### Is there an entrance exam for MBA admission?
Yes. MBA applicants need a valid CAT, MAT, XAT or GMAT score, or they can take the
university's own management aptitude test. Shortlisted candidates are invited to a
group discussion and personal interview.

### Can international students apply?
Yes. International students apply through the International Office. They need a
passport, academic transcripts with an equivalence certificate from the Association
of Indian Universities (AIU), proof of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80 or
equivalent) and a student visa. Application deadline for international students is 30 April.

## Fees and Scholarships

### What is the tuition fee for B.Tech?
The tuition fee for B.Tech is INR 1,80,000 per semester. Additional charges include a
one-time admission fee of INR 25,000 and an examination fee of INR 5,000 per semester.
Hostel and mess charges are separate.

### How can I pay my fees?
Fees can be paid online through the Student Portal (net banking, UPI, debit/credit card)
or by demand draft in favour of the university payable at the city of the main campus.
Semester fees are due by the 15th of the first month of each semester. A late fee of
INR 100 per day applies after the due date.

### What scholarships are available?
- **Merit scholarship:** 25–100% tuition waiver for students scoring 90%+ in Class 12 or
  ranking in the top 1% of the entrance test.
- **Sports scholarship:** up to 50% waiver for state/national-level athletes.
- **Need-based scholarship:** up to 40% waiver for families with annual income below INR 6 lakh.
- **Sibling concession:** 10% waiver for the second sibling enrolled at the university.
Scholarships are renewed each year if the student maintains a CGPA of 7.5 or above and
has no disciplinary record.

### What is the fee refund policy?
If a student withdraws before the start of classes, 90% of the tuition fee is refunded
(minus a processing fee of INR 1,000). Withdrawal within 15 days of the start of classes
gives an 80% refund, within 30 days 50%, and after 30 days no refund is given.
Refunds are processed within 30 working days.

## Academics

### What is the minimum attendance requirement?
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in every course to be eligible to sit the
end-semester examination. Medical leave supported by a certificate may be condoned up to
an additional 10% with the Dean's approval.

### How is the CGPA calculated?
Each course carries credits and a letter grade on a 10-point scale (O=10, A+=9, A=8,
B+=7, B=6, C=5, P=4, F=0). SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points in a
semester; CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all semesters completed.
Percentage equivalent = CGPA × 9.5.

### What is NTCC?
NTCC (Non-Teaching Credit Courses) are credit-bearing activities that are not taught
in a classroom: summer internships, term papers, minor and major projects, and
industry training. NTCC work is evaluated through a report, a presentation and a viva
by a faculty guide and an external examiner. Students must complete all NTCC
requirements of their programme to graduate.

### How do I register for NTCC / internships?
Students register for NTCC through the Student Portal at the start of the semester,
select a faculty guide, and submit a synopsis within two weeks. For summer internships,
the Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) shares company opportunities; students may also
arrange their own internship with prior approval from the department.

### What happens if I fail a course?
You can appear in the supplementary (back) examination held in June/July or re-register
for the course in a later semester. The supplementary exam fee is INR 1,500 per course.
The maximum grade after a supplementary exam is capped at B.

### How do I apply for re-evaluation of my answer sheet?
Apply on the Student Portal within 7 days of result declaration and pay INR 1,000 per
course. Re-evaluated results are published within 21 days. If marks increase by more
than 10%, the fee is refunded.

## Campus Life

### Is hostel accommodation available?
Yes, separate hostels for boys and girls are available on campus with single, double and
triple sharing rooms. Annual charges range from INR 1,10,000 (triple sharing, non-AC) to
INR 2,20,000 (single, AC), including mess. Rooms are allotted on a first-come,
first-served basis after fee payment.

### What are the library timings?
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and from 9:00 AM to
5:00 PM on weekends. During examination weeks it stays open 24/7. Students can borrow
up to 4 books for 14 days; the fine for late return is INR 5 per book per day.

### Are there student clubs and societies?
The university has more than 40 clubs: technical (robotics, coding, IEEE student branch),
cultural (music, dance, drama), literary, entrepreneurship cell, NSS and NCC. Club
registrations open in the first two weeks of August.

### Does the university provide transport?
University buses run on 25 routes across the city. The annual transport fee is
INR 30,000 and passes are issued through the Transport Office after online registration.

### Is there medical support on campus?
A 24×7 health centre with a resident doctor and nurses operates on campus. An ambulance
is available for emergencies. All students are covered by group medical insurance of
up to INR 1,00,000 per year.

## Placements

### What placement support does the university provide?
The Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) organises campus recruitment drives, pre-placement
talks, mock interviews, aptitude training and resume workshops. Students must register
with CRC in their pre-final year and maintain a CGPA of at least 6.0 with no active
backlogs to be eligible.

### What were last year's placement statistics?
In the last placement season, 86% of eligible B.Tech students were placed. The highest
package offered was INR 42 LPA and the average package was INR 7.2 LPA. Over 350
companies participated in campus recruitment.
